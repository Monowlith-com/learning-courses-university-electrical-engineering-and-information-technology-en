# University – Electrical Engineering and Information Technology – Sources {#quellenverzeichnis}

This directory brings together curricular evidence and teaching sources for the complete Bachelor’s area, the Bachelor–Master bridge, Master’s specialisations and the research and final project. Teaching sources are assigned to the lessons in which they are used.
