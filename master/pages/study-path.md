# University – Electrical Engineering and Information Technology – Study Path {#studienweg}

The modules progress from electrical networks to demonstrably functioning technical systems. Mathematics and computer science provide methods for modelling and simulation. This study path is intended for university-level self-study.

## M1 – Linear Electrical Networks and Circuit Theory {#S1}

Begin with the sign convention. Progress from resistor networks through systematic circuit analysis to energy storage components, alternating current and resonance.

1. [M1-L1 – Charge, Current, Voltage and Power](../M1-L1.md)
2. [M1-L2 – Resistance, Ohm's Law and Simple Networks](../M1-L2.md)
3. [M1-L3 – Kirchhoff's Laws and Systematic Network Analysis](../M1-L3.md)
4. [M1-L4 – Equivalent Circuits and Maximum Power Transfer](../M1-L4.md)
5. [M1-L5 – Capacitors, Inductors and Transient Responses](../M1-L5.md)
6. [M1-L6 – Sinusoidal Alternating Current and Complex Impedance](../M1-L6.md)
7. [M1-L7 – AC Power, Frequency Response and Resonance](../M1-L7.md)
